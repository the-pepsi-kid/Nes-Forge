# Pose and artwork navigation acceptance

1. Open the new standalone HTML and your supported Punch-Out ROM/native project.
   Click Recovered content. Confirm Native pose positions · Glass Joe is expanded
   above the ordinary source Pose picker/preview. Select incremental $C0 · idle
   and Edit native Glass Joe pose position.
2. Click CHR SHEET. Confirm Sprite Bank and Glass Joe's individual tiles appear.
   The strip above the sheet should name pose $C0 and native bank 29. Choose BG
   Bank manually, then Show pose sprite tiles. Confirm the art returns.
3. Choose Edit native CHR bank 29. Confirm the dedicated physical Scene and BG
   Bank are selected. Paint one recognizable pixel; Undo/Redo and Save Project
   As/reopen. Build & Run or export/reimport and inspect the original game.
4. Return with Open native pose position. Earlier reference art is a snapshot;
   inspect changed pixels in the native physical bank, a fresh fight reference
   or current-build replay. Check Keep idle poses together, set X=-12 from -14,
   leave Y=-97 and Apply. Confirm grouped position and art changes build together.
5. Check Retro/Resources/Connected Workspace and your unrelated saved projects.
   Check Audio, screen size, device input and an independent emulator as needed.
   Those observations are owner acceptance, not established by parsed-DOM tests.
