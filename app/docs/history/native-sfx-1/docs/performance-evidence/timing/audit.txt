CONFIRMED BEHAVIOR / CONDITIONAL LIMITATIONS
1. src/legacy/01-emulator.js:578-615: elapsed-time scheduler targets 60.0988 Hz,
   admits at most 2 frames per callback, breaks after a 10 ms processing slice,
   and discards all remaining whole frame debts. It caps accumulated debt at 4
   frames. This avoids unlimited catch-up but can discard useful catch-up work.
   Synthetic 10 seconds, callback rate 30 Hz, frame cost 11 ms: 300 emulated
   frames instead of the ideal 600, 300 recorded dropped debts, 99 audio underruns.
   At 30 Hz / 4 ms per frame: 600 frames, 0 dropped debts, 0 audio underruns.
   At 50 Hz / 11 ms per frame: 500 frames, 100 dropped debts, 38 audio underruns.
   These conditions demonstrate that the policy itself can cause slower game
   time and insufficient samples. Whether a real device reaches them is unknown.
   At 30 Hz two 11 ms frames would fit in a 33.3 ms interval before other costs,
   but increasing the budget unconditionally could hurt UI responsiveness.

2. Same actual scheduler tested at 60, 90, 120, 144 Hz and 4 ms/frame: expected
   600 frames over 10 seconds, no audio underruns. No simple high-refresh-rate
   double-speed defect reproduced. Do not claim a fixed one-frame-per-rAF bug.

3. src/legacy/01-emulator.js:827-839: output intentionally begins/resumes with
   45 ms lead. A per-frame buffer lasts about 16.64 ms. Flush considers fewer
   than 4 ms remaining to be an underrun; it resets scheduling to 45 ms. It
   clears queued sources above 120 ms of existing lead or at 12 live sources.
   The reported max queue can exceed 120 ms by the newly appended buffer, so
   120 ms is a resync trigger rather than a hard final queue-length maximum.
   No unbounded buffer growth or buffer-copy corruption reproduced.

4. src/legacy/01-emulator.js:617-624: HUD frame cost includes emuRunFrame but
   excludes apuFlush and renderEmu (including sidebar DOM work). Controlled
   4 ms frame + 5 ms render still reports frame 4 ms. It reports last-frame cost,
   not median/P95 total work. Queue depth excludes device/output latency.
   A 250 ms stall executes 2 frames and reports only 2 dropped, although about
   15 frames of wall time elapsed: the initial accumulator cap is not counted.
   Therefore existing dropped count is not a total wall-time loss metric.

5. src/legacy/01-emulator.js:815-824,843-848: retry calls resume only for state
   'suspended'. A synthetic context in state 'interrupted' keeps zero scheduled
   audio samples and receives zero resume calls. This is a conditional branch
   gap, not evidence of a specific phone/browser interruption. Verify actual
   state values and resume behavior on affected browser before changing it.

6. src/legacy/01-emulator.js:843-848: Mute changes master gain; APU calculation,
   sample generation, AudioBuffer allocation and scheduling continue. Muting
   may reveal perception differences but cannot isolate audio CPU overhead.

7. src/legacy/01-emulator.js:636,1029-1051: every rendered emulation frame writes
   CPU register text, PPU/sidebar fields and replaces a 32-swatch palette HTML
   string, even while the play panel is expanded and that inspector is hidden.
   This is confirmed extra work, but its contribution to real mobile lag is
   unmeasured. src/services/emulator-play-ui.js refreshes 5 times/second and
   measures/writes expanded canvas fitting on each refresh, another measurable
   host-side cost rather than evidence the emulator core needs rewriting.

src/legacy/02-interaction-bootstrap.js:44 pauses on document.hidden. Source

SMALLEST USEFUL NEXT STEP
Capture one comparable 15-30 second session in embedded and expanded Play view
on the affected phone, same ROM, audio output route, browser and device state.
Record display callback cadence/gaps, emulated frames per elapsed second,
emuRunFrame cost, apuFlush cost, render cost, queue current/min/max, underrun
and resync deltas, AudioContext state/rate/baseLatency/outputLatency if exposed,
visibility/focus/resize events, and exact application + ROM hashes. Avoid
recording project contents or wallet/device-controller identifiers unnecessarily.
Use host-boundary instrumentation only; do not edit CPU/PPU/APU implementations.

BOUNDED FIX CANDIDATES AFTER MEASUREMENT
- If hidden inspector rendering is material, throttle or skip inspector updates
  during expanded play while preserving canvas presentation and an immediate
  refresh on return. Do not skip CPU/PPU emulation to improve visual FPS.
- If the 10 ms catch-up policy reproduces, evaluate a bounded cadence-aware
  host budget/debt policy with 30/60/90/120/144 Hz plus long-stall tests. Keep a
  maximum work bound and generation protections. This would be a targeted host
  timing change in a protected legacy file, requiring careful scoped review.
- If audio interruptions reproduce, add narrowly scoped recoverable-state retry
  on user activation, verified for that browser. Do not call resume continuously.
- Do not reduce the 45 ms lead blindly; that trades latency for more underruns.
